# Long description (draft, English)

Not submitted. At most 4000 characters.

Velunor is an idle adventure. You open chests, build a team and walk a road, including while the game is closed. The save stays on the device.

Chests are loot boxes. They cost gold or marks earned in the game. No purchases.

The game also has a codex, a mastery tree, a research tower and expeditions. Fights are fantasy fights with types and numbers, and no blood.

When a connection is up: arena, friends, trading, a guild and a shared beast. Guild chat and chat with friends are open from age 13, with a filter, reports, blocks and moderation. Names are filtered and can be reported. Online features require an internet connection. Without a network, single-player still works.

The wheel pays marks and costs gold earned in the game. The stakes cannot be bought.

No purchases. No advertising.
